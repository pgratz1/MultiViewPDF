\section*{Coupled Widget Assembly}
\label{sec:top}
A widget assembly is disclosed. As shown in FIG. 1, the widget 10 is coupled to a frame 12 by a resilient member 14. See U.S. Pat. No. 5,123,456 to Smith et al. (2001) at col. 3, lines 4--5, for a comparable arrangement. The resilient member 14 may be made of steel, e.g. spring steel, or of an elastomer.

Further details are available online at \url{https://www.uspto.gov} and in the MuPDF documentation at \href{https://mupdf.readthedocs.io/en/latest/}{mupdf.readthedocs.io}. The claims begin on page~\pageref{sec:claims} (see \hyperref[sec:claims]{the claims section}).
